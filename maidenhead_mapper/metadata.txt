description=Amateur radio maps: Maidenhead grid layers, PSK Reporter station maps with an activity timeline, and a live WSJT-X map.
about=For amateur (ham) radio operators.
    Create Maidenhead grid: correctly aligned field (2-character) and square (4-character) polygon layers, styled and labelled.
    Fetch from PSK Reporter / Import PSK Reporter reports: one marker per station you heard or that heard you, with report count, SNR, bands, modes, times and distance/bearing. Style by SNR, band or direction.
    Import WSJT-X ALL.TXT: past sessions as stations and exchange lines (both reports, your SNR of each side).
    Activity timeline: a reports-per-hour chart for any of these; drag across it to map only those hours.
    WSJT-X live map: listens to WSJT-X's UDP feed (unicast, or multicast alongside JTAlert/GridTracker) and plots decoded stations as they are heard, fading with age. Logged QSOs show as stars.
    Ionosphere: check any paths against KC2G's current MUF/foF2/hmF2 maps: path MUF, hops at the measured layer height, and which contacts F2 can't explain.
    No extra Python packages needed.
